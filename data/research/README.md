# KU Leuven gait research dataset

De Mulder, Tine; Adams, Heleen; Dewit, Tijl; Molenaers, Guy; Van Campenhout, Anja; Desloovere, Kaat (2024). *A Comparison of the Immediate Effects of Verbal and Virtual Reality Feedback on Gait in Children with Cerebral Palsy*. KU Leuven Research Data Repository, version 1.0.

- Original dataset and citation: https://doi.org/10.48804/JUNNWW
- Dataset license: [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

The six CSV files and the accompanying data dictionary are redistributed unchanged for this non-commercial research demo. Participant codes are anonymized in the original release.
